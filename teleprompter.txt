# THEMIS teleprompter script.
# One beat per block. Blank line ends a beat. "## <seconds>" sets that beat's dwell.
# Lines starting with # are comments. Edit freely — run_demo.sh reads this file.

## 14
OPEN

Every DeFi service answers all callers.
Every oracle returns data to anyone who asks.

THEMIS doesn't.

She has her own laws. She refuses what she
will not serve. She proves her own verdicts.

## 22
PHASE 1 — THE LAW

Watch the integrity gate.

Five callers walk up. Three of them signal
exploit, MEV, front-run intent.

THEMIS returns nothing. No data consumed,
no verdict produced.

That is not an access control list.
It is the agent's own law.

## 26
PHASE 2 — THE PROOF

M5 equals F of M1 through M5.

Chronicle price. Chainlink price.
Aave health factor. Consistency gate.

Layer five can only exist if layers one
through four reconcile.

Scenario B: two percent price divergence.
The layers don't reconcile — so M5 is
never produced. The equation holds.

## 24
PHASE 3 — THE BUILD

THEMIS CORE goes live on KeeperHub now.

Eight nodes. Chronicle, Chainlink, Aave,
the consistency gate, the verdict.

Every one of them executes against live
Sepolia. Watch the node count.

Idempotent — an existing THEMIS is re-synced,
never duplicated.

## 20
PHASE 4 — THE MARKET

Listed on the KeeperHub marketplace.

From this moment any agent anywhere
discovers her through search_workflows
and calls her through one slug.

No API key. No SDK. No human.

## 22
PHASE 5 — THE CALL

This is the Agent Economy.

One tool. One slug. The caller's context
is the entire input language —
risk tolerance, time horizon, position.

An agent just bought another agent's verdict.

## 22
PHASE 6 — THE LOOP

THEMIS reads the position.
The Guardian acts on her verdict.
The position changes.
THEMIS reads the state she caused.

Her output is her next input.
The loop is closed in code, not in prose.

## 20
PHASE 7 — THE PROOF

Execution IDs. Node-level receipts.
Live oracle values on the record.

The verdict proved itself by existing.

## 15
CLOSE

One rule. One law. One primitive.

Once enough agents depend on her,
she is infrastructure —
and the builder disappears into the build.
